About NameForge

NameForge is a small tool built for one purpose: turning a plain name into something worth showing off. Whether it's a Free Fire tag, an Instagram bio, or a Discord handle, the right styled name says something about you before you say a word.

Instead of pulling from a fixed list of pre-written names, NameForge combines unicode letterforms with symbols and ornaments live, in your browser, every time you press "Forge names." That means the combinations you see are generated fresh rather than repeated from a static database.

The project is independently built and maintained. It doesn't collect accounts, passwords, or personal information — you type a name, you copy a result, that's the whole interaction.

Have feedback or found a symbol that doesn't render correctly on your device? Reach out through the contact page.
