Questions people ask

Is NameForge free to use?

Yes. Generating and copying styled names costs nothing and doesn't require an account.

Will these names work in Free Fire, PUBG or Instagram?

Most platforms accept unicode characters in names and bios. A few very long ornament combinations may be trimmed by character limits, so shorter results tend to be the safest bet for in-game names.

Why do I get different results each time?

NameForge doesn't pull from a fixed list — it combines fonts and symbols live, so pressing "Forge names" again gives you a new set to choose from.

Some symbols show as boxes on my phone. Why?

That means your device's font doesn't include that particular unicode character yet. The text is still valid and will usually display correctly once pasted into apps like Instagram or Free Fire.
